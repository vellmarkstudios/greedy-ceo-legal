## 1. WHO WE ARE

GREEDY CEO ("the Game", "we", "us", "our") is a mobile idle/incremental game operated by **Vellmark Studios**, based in the United States.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: **vellmarkstudios@gmail.com**

## 2. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information you provide directly:
- **Nickname**: a short display name (3–12 characters) you choose to identify yourself in-game.
- **Birth year** (year only, not full date): used for age verification under COPPA and GDPR.
- **Country**: detected automatically from your device timezone, or you may manually choose a country in your profile settings.
- **Google account email** (only if you optionally choose to "Link Google Account"): for cross-device cloud save synchronization.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically:
- **Approximate country**: detected from your device's timezone and language settings (no GPS).
- **Game progress**: in-game money, businesses owned, achievements unlocked, settings.
- **Device identifier**: a randomly generated ID stored locally on your device. This ID is NOT linked to your real identity.
- **Crash reports**: if the game crashes, basic error information helps us diagnose bugs.
- **Advertising ID** (Android): used by Google AdMob for ad serving (you can reset/disable in device settings).

### 2.3 Information we do NOT collect:
- Real name
- Phone number
- Physical address
- Payment information (handled directly by Google Play, not by us)
- Photos, contacts, or files
- GPS / precise location data
- Microphone or camera data
- Browsing history outside the Game

## 6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA)

We are committed to protecting children's privacy under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

- During first launch, we ask for the player's birth year (year only).
- Players who indicate they are **under 13 years old** have ALL chat and social features automatically disabled.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is strictly necessary for safe gameplay.
- Parents who believe their child has shared personal information may contact us at **vellmarkstudios@gmail.com** to request deletion. We will respond within 30 days.

## 7. YOUR RIGHTS

### GDPR (EU users):
You have the right to:
- **Access**: request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Correct**: request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Delete**: request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Export**: request your data in a portable format (JSON).
- **Object**: object to certain processing.
- **Restrict**: request restriction of processing.
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection authority.

### CCPA (California residents):
You have the right to:
- Know what categories of personal information we collect.
- Know whether your personal information is sold or disclosed and to whom (we do NOT sell).
- Opt out of any "sale" of personal information.
- Access your personal information.
- Request deletion of your personal information.
- Equal service and price, even if you exercise your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact **vellmarkstudios@gmail.com** with your nickname. We will respond within 30 days.

## 8. HOW TO DELETE YOUR DATA

You can delete your game data at any time through any of these methods:

- **In-game**: Settings → "DELETE SAVE" button. This wipes your local progress.
- **Email request**: send an email to **vellmarkstudios@gmail.com** with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your in-game nickname. We will delete your data within 30 days and send confirmation.
- **Uninstall**: uninstalling the app removes local data, but your cloud profile may persist. Use email request for full deletion.

## 9. DATA RETENTION
